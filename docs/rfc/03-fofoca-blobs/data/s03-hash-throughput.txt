S0.3 — hash and outboard-construction throughput
Captured 2026-08-03. M-series Mac, 10 cores, Rust 1.95.0.
blake3 1.8.5, bao-tree 0.16.0.

================================================================
NATIVE  (aarch64, NEON; 256 MiB input, best of 5)
================================================================

blake3 (the ceiling — known to use the wide SIMD path):
  blake3::hash single-threaded                    2359.5 MiB/s   (108.5 ms)
  blake3 update_rayon (multithreaded)            12601.9 MiB/s   ( 20.3 ms)

bao-tree outboard construction (what we actually pay):
  PreOrderMemOutboard::create @ 16 KiB groups     2240.6 MiB/s   (114.3 ms)
  PreOrderMemOutboard::create @ 64 KiB groups     2256.6 MiB/s   (113.4 ms)

verdict:
  outboard@16K / blake3 ceiling = 0.95x
  outboard@64K / blake3 ceiling = 0.96x
  rayon / single-threaded blake3 = 5.34x

A ratio near 1.0 means bao-tree rides blake3's wide SIMD path.
A ratio near 1/8 (~0.12) on AVX2 or 1/4 (~0.25) on NEON would have meant
chunk-by-chunk hashing. It does not.

CAVEAT: the 5.34x rayon figure is NOT available to outboard construction.
blake3's `rayon` feature only adds `update_rayon` / `update_mmap_rayon`, its
whole-input APIs; bao-tree walks the tree itself. 2.2 GiB/s single-threaded is
the rate that actually applies (~0.45 s per GiB).

================================================================
WASM  (node/V8; 64 MiB input, best of 5)
================================================================

  variant    blake3 hash     outboard@16K    outboard@64K
  portable   1197.5 MiB/s    1191.0 MiB/s    1183.3 MiB/s
  simd       2160.0 MiB/s    2042.5 MiB/s    2121.8 MiB/s

  simd128 speedup:  hash 1.80x   outboard@16K 1.72x   outboard@64K 1.79x

Wasm + SIMD reaches 2160 / 2359 = 92% of native single-threaded blake3.

CAVEAT: measured under node/V8, not a browser engine. Safari/JavaScriptCore may
differ. Also note 02-performance.md cites "6x on large inputs under Wasmtime"
for blake3's wasm32_simd; that does not reproduce here — 1.80x under V8.
Engine choice evidently matters a great deal for this number.
